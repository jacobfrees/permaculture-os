# 09 — GOVERNANCE

Governance where power is exercised by **everyone together** — held by no
person and no group. There is no separation between one member and another.

## Documents

- **[COVENANT.md](COVENANT.md)** — the one-page covenant every member signs
- **[GOVERNANCE-MANUAL.md](GOVERNANCE-MANUAL.md)** — how the covenant is kept:
  circles, consent decisions, rotation, transparency, conflict, law relations,
  annual audit

## Why These Are Designed This Way

This governance is **cult-proof by construction** and **government-friendly by
policy**:

- No founder authority — the founder is first worker, not leader
- Every role rotates; every critical system is two-deep
- Exit is sacred: leaving is free, at any time, with everything that is yours
- Dissent is infrastructure: a formal Devil's Advocate, one reasoned objection
  pauses any decision, banned-patterns list anyone can invoke
- Total transparency: finances, decisions, teachings — all public
- Neutral on the sacred: no required belief, ritual, diet, or dress
- The law of the land is followed fully; sovereignty schemes are disqualifying

## The Anti-Cult Self-Audit (annual, with a sister community's outside eyes)

1. Is any one person irreplaceable anywhere?
2. Can every member leave today with zero friction?
3. Was every objection heard, logged, worked with?
4. Are finances accurate, dual-authorized, public?
5. Do members have active outside relationships and income?
6. Is any knowledge unavailable to any member?
7. Is dissent comfortable right now?
8. Has any structure begun separating members from one another?

Any failing answer **pauses all expansion until fixed**.
