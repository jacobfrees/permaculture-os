# THE COVENANT
### [COMMUNITY NAME] — v0.1 (forkable template)

*We gather under this covenant freely, and any of us may leave under it freely, at any time.*

---

**We commit to one another as equals.**

There are no leaders and no followers here. There are no ranks, no inner circles, and no one above a question. Power is not held by any person and not held by any group — it is exercised by everyone together, through consent, and it belongs to no one. Every member is a full director of this community and of their own life within it. What one knows, all may learn. What one decides, all must consent to. Between one of us and another, there is no separation.

**We commit to meeting every real need we can.**

Food, clean water, shelter, warmth and cooling, sanitation, energy, learning, meaningful work, and honest relationship — for every member, without exception and without hierarchy. We build these from the land, from each other, and from open knowledge shared freely with the world.

**We commit to integrity.**

We act from integrity, authentically — in our dealings with each other, with our neighbors, and with the laws and officials of the land in which we live. We follow the rules and laws of our locality, fully and without evasion. We pay what we owe, file what is required, and welcome scrutiny at all times.

**We commit to openness.**

Our finances are public. Our decisions are recorded. Our teachings are public. There is no secret knowledge, no inner doctrine, no advanced level. Anyone may read everything we know at any time.

**We commit to freedom of conscience and connection.**

No belief, ritual, diet, dress, or practice is required here. Each member's faith, family, friends, work outside this community, and life beyond it are their own and are honored by all. We strengthen outward bonds; we never restrict them.

**We commit to the freedom to leave.**

Any member may leave at any time, with their property, their savings, and their relationships intact — without shame, penalty, or obstacle. If leaving ever feels impossible to any member, the whole community stops to correct that, immediately, as our highest-priority failure.

**We commit to dissent.**

A single well-reasoned objection pauses any decision. Questions are welcomed, assigned, and rewarded. Doubt is a duty here, not a disloyalty.
