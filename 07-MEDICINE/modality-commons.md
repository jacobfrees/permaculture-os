# 07-MEDICINE — The Modality Commons
*Every health tradition, every healer, every practice — demonstrated openly,
in person, so every person gets exposure to everything. Open source wide and
in-person deep.*

## The Principle

No single health modality is complete — and no one should have to choose one
blindly, or travel the world for years to discover what exists. So the
community network becomes **a living museum of health modalities where the
exhibits are real people doing real healing, in front of you, and teaching
you.**

The kernel rule: **demonstrate, document, share back.** Every modality in the
commons is presented in person by a practitioner AND documented openly so it
travels the network.

## The Modality Roster (the exposure list)

Each community hosts what it can; the network collectively carries all of it.
Travelers rotate — the WWOOF/Worldpacker exchange brings a different modality
through every month.

- Movement: yoga, qigong/tai chi, functional fitness, dance, structural integration
- Breathwork, meditation, sleep hygiene, circadian health

## How the Commons Runs

### The Residency Model
- Practitioners apply for **modality residencies**: 2 weeks–3 months living at
  a community in exchange for demonstration + teaching + documentation
- Their room/board comes from the traveler exchange (same as clinicians);
  some practitioners earn modest stipends via the commons fund or their own
  client work (their license, their income — never pooled)
- **The monthly modality calendar** — each community publishes what's being
  demonstrated this month; travelers plan pilgrimages; mesh carries the
  calendar network-wide

### The Documentation Standard (open source wide)
Every modality demonstrated at a community produces:
1. **A practice file** in the kernel: what it is, claimed mechanisms, safety
   profile, contraindications, what it costs to learn, where it came from
2. **An honest evidence note** — what research exists, what's anecdote; we
   document traditions respectfully AND truthfully. Modality docs are
   written like open-source READMEs: "what this claims, what's verified,
   who should NOT use it"
3. **Video/photo instruction** where the practitioner permits (some
   ceremonial traditions are demonstrated in person ONLY by choice — that
   choice is honored; the commons records its existence, not its secrets)
4. **A safety gate** — anything with real risk (fasting, sweatlodges,
   aggressive bodywork) carries screening protocols; the T1–T4 tiering from
   the universal healthcare doc maps which modalities need licensed oversight

### The In-Person Rule (open source deep)
Documentation spreads knowledge; **residency spreads discernment.** You can
read about acupuncture anywhere — feeling the needle placement, watching a
master read a face, sensing whether a practice resonates with YOUR body:
that requires people. The design: **every member should encounter, in person,
every major modality at least once a year** — via local practitioners, or
via the traveler rotation, or via modality festivals (below).

### The Modality Festival (twice yearly, network-wide)
- Communities take turns hosting a gathering where 10–30 practitioners
  demonstrate simultaneously — the permaculture convergence meets a health fair
- Travelers/members rotate through experiences; the annual "what worked for
  me" forum feeds real outcome reports back to the protocol layer
- This is also the recruiting event for practitioners — the commons grows by
  demonstration, exactly like the community itself

## The Safety & Integrity Layer (non-negotiable)

1. **The harm line:** modalities that delay legitimate emergency/critical care
   get flagged; the T1–T4 tiering tells every member when a modality is
   complementary vs. when conventional care is the only answer. Everyone
   trained in T1 basics knows the red flags (stroke, sepsis, heart) that
   bypass every modality and go straight to emergency services
2. **No required modality:** choosing NONE of the spiritual/energetic
   offerings is a fully respected path — Covenant neutrality applies to
   health practices as to religion
3. **Practitioner accountability:** each practitioner's residency reviews are
   public in the ledger; harm reports go to a standing review circle;
   practitioners who harm get documented, and where they broke law/licensure,
   reported
4. **Vulnerable-person protection:** children, elders, and dependent adults
   get the same modality exposure only with guardian consent and additional
   safeguarding — the community's child protection standards apply to all
   bodywork and ceremony
5. **No modality sells its way to power:** practitioners may charge for
   personal services transparently, but modality status in the commons is
   earned by demonstration and documentation — never by payment. (Same rule
   as the reciprocity ledger: contribution earns standing, money doesn't)

## The Nordic Connection, Distributed

Nordic well-being works because saunas, nature, community, and meaning are
*normalized infrastructure*. The modality commons normalizes the same
worldwide: thermal culture, nature practice, movement, breath, community
counseling — not as weekend novelties but as the weekly texture of life.
The Nordic model needs a small homogeneous country; **the commons needs
neither** — it runs the well-being layer anywhere, through people.

## Kernel Verdict

- Every modality gets demonstrated **in person by real people** (the
  discernment layer) and documented **openly** (the knowledge layer)
- The traveler exchange that already moves a million people/year becomes the
  modality circulation system — no one community needs to hold everything;
  the network holds everything, and each site holds some of it well
- Exposure is universal, choice is absolute, safety is tiered, evidence is
  honest, and no practitioner's money buys standing
- Members become fluent in their own health — the deepest version of "director
  of your own reality"
