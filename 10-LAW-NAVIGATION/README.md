# 10 — LAW NAVIGATION

**We follow the laws of the land. Fully.** Compliance is the strategy — this
module exists to make compliance achievable for ordinary people.

## The Doors

| Topic | The realistic paths |
|---|---|
| **Land** | community land trusts; agricultural zoning; rural counties with owner-builder friendly codes |
| **Buildings** | owner-builder permits; agricultural exemptions; alternative-materials provisions (engineer-stamped templates included) |
| **Water** | rainwater capture rights vary enormously — per-jurisdiction registry, community-maintained |
| **Legal form** | unincorporated association → cooperative → nonprofit/land trust as resources appear (templates included) |
| **Relations** | introduce the project to county officials early and openly — most conflicts are prevention failures |

## Disqualifying Behavior (per the Covenant)

- Tax protest schemes
- False identities
- "Sovereign citizen" claims
- Concealment from officials

These endanger every member and violate the Covenant's integrity commitment.

## The Community Legal Atlas

Each community files its legal learnings back here: *"here's how we did it in
[county/state/country]"* — permits filed, what worked, what officials asked
for, costs, timelines. Over time this becomes a world atlas of compliant
pathways, so no community anywhere has to start from zero.
