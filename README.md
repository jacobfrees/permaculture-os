# 🌍 Permaculture OS

**An open-source operating system for regenerative communities.**

One person can start it. Any climate can host it. Every community that runs it
makes every other community stronger.

> There is no one starving in the world because of a lack of knowledge.
> That is the goal. This repository is the mechanism.

## What is this?

Permaculture OS is a complete, forkable **kernel** of knowledge, designs,
governance, and protocols for starting a self-sufficient permaculture community
anywhere on Earth — built from local materials, maintained by open-source
collaboration, and connected to every other community running the same kernel.

It works like an operating system:

- **The Kernel** — universal patterns that work in every climate
  (water first, soil factories, food forests, earth shelter, governance)
- **Drivers** — climate-specific adaptations (arid, tropical, temperate, cold, coastal, highland)
- **Protocols** — how communities connect, sync knowledge, route surplus, and help each other
- **Translations** — every language, offline-first, diagram-first

## Core Principles

1. **Executable by one untrained person with local materials** — or it doesn't ship
2. **Diagrams over paragraphs** — diagrams translate across every language for free
3. **No separation** — governance where power is exercised by everyone together,
   held by no person and no group (see `09-GOVERNANCE/COVENANT.md`)
4. **Follow the laws of the land, fully** — compliance is the strategy
5. **Fork, adapt, share back** — every community's hard-won lesson becomes every
   community's starting point

## The Covenant (one-page summary)

> We commit to one another as equals. There are no leaders and no followers
> here. Power is not held by any person and not held by any group — it is
> exercised by everyone together. Every member may leave at any time with
> everything that is theirs. Our finances, decisions, and teachings are public.
> We act from integrity, authentically — with each other, with our neighbors,
> and with the laws of the land.

Read the full covenant: [`09-GOVERNANCE/COVENANT.md`](09-GOVERNANCE/COVENANT.md)

## Who is this for?

- **A founder** who wants to start a community from nothing — begin at `00-START-HERE/`
- **An existing homestead** that wants to connect to the network and share what works
- **A mature community** ready to federate, mentor, and seed new sites
- **A translator, engineer, teacher, lawyer, or gardener** — every module needs maintainers
