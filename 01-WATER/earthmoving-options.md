# 01-WATER — Earthmoving Options: Hand to Machine
*The escalation ladder. Choose the smallest tool that finishes in your season.*

## The Core Geometry Trick

Permaculture earthworks need almost zero dirt-*moving* — they need dirt-*flipping*.
Swales, berms, and terraces are **cut-and-fill**: dig the ditch, throw it forward onto the berm.
Dirt travels 1–2 feet, not 200 yards. This is why humans built terraces for centuries
without machines — and why one person can build a working water system.

## The Ladder

### Level 0 — Pure Hand (≈$100 tools)
| Scale | Capacity |
|---|---|
| 1 person, steady day | 1.5–2 m³ (2.5–3 tons) dug and placed |
| 1 person, sprint | ~3 m³ |
| 6-person crew | 10–12 m³/day |
| 20-person work party | 35–40 m³/day |

**Tools:** mattock, long-handled flat shovel, A-frame level + string, wheelbarrow, 5-gal buckets.
**Force multipliers:** cut-and-fill design; 30 min/day beats monthly marathons; dig 12–24h
after rain (dirt moves 2x easier); line crews along contour; terraced dig positions (dig at
waist height, never below knees); stomp + water compaction.

### Level 1 — Mini Skid / Dingo-class (walk-behind)
- **Output:** 15–25 yd³/day
- **Rental:** $200–300/day, $800–1,100/week | **Fuel:** $10–15/day
- Tight spots, small swales, grading pads.

### Level 2 — Mini Excavator (1.5–3.5t) ★ permaculture favorite
- **Output:** 30–50 yd³/day
- **Rental:** $250–350/day, $900–1,300/week | **Fuel:** $10–20/day
- Digs every swale, pond, and trench on a 1–3 acre site in one week.

### Level 3 — Skid Steer (Bobcat)
- **Output:** 30–60 yd³/day (pushing/spreading; mediocre at hard digging)
- **Rental:** $250–400/day, $1,000–1,500/week | **Fuel:** $15–25/day
- Attachments: auger, trencher, grapple (+$40–80/day each).

### Level 4 — Compact Tractor + Loader/Box Blade
- **Output:** 25–45 yd³/day
- **Rental:** $200–350/day, $800–1,300/week | **Fuel:** $15–25/day
- The homestead multitasker; box blade is the real "push dirt mover" for roads/pads.

### Level 5 — Full-Size Excavator (8–20t)
- **Output:** 100–200 yd³/day
- **Rental:** $450–800/day, $1,800–3,200/week | **Fuel:** $30–60/day
- Big ponds, dam spillways, major terracing in days.

### Level 6 — Bulldozer (D3–D5)
- **Output:** 150–300 yd³/day
- **Rental:** $500–900/day, $2,000–3,500/week | **Fuel:** $40–80/day
- Long-distance pushing, dam cores, road cutting.

### Level 7 — Backhoe Loader
- **Output:** 50–80 yd³/day | **Rental:** $350–500/day | **Fuel:** $25–40/day
- Dig + load + push in one machine.

## Support Cast (easy to forget)
- **Dump/tilt trailer:** $70–120/day — moving dirt *between* piles
- **Trencher:** $150–250/day — overflow pipes and water lines in hours
- **Plate compactor:** $40–60/day — essential for berms/earthbag
- **Delivery fees:** $100–200 each way — rent by the WEEK, not three separate days

*Figures are US averages, realistic sustained output with average operator on mixed soil.
Clay halves output; loose sand can double it. Local prices vary ±30%.*

## The Hybrid Strategy (recommended default)

1. **HAND:** contour marking, test holes, swale lips, spillways — the precision work.
   Doubles as crew training and community-building (digging parties recruit).
2. **RENT:** one focused machine week does all heavy cutting at once.
   Mini excavator week ≈ $900–1,300 + skid steer 2–3 days ≈ $600–1,000
   → **entire 1–3 acre water system ≈ $2,000–2,500**.
3. **HAND:** shape, stomp-compact, plant berms — the soil-building work.

Hand-built earth is contour-perfect, thoroughly compacted, and builds the founding
crew into a team. The machine week is scheduled AFTER hand-training.

## What One Person Can Build (no machine, one season)

- 4–6 swales (~300m) on 2–3 acres
- One 15–20 m³ pocket pond over 2–3 months of mornings
- Full earthbag shelter pad
- ~1,000+ m³ of earth shaped in a year — a small excavator's two weeks, for $0 fuel
