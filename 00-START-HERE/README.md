# 00 — START HERE

**You are one person. This works. Here is the sequence.**

## The Principle

You do not recruit people to build a community. You build a visible, working
abundance, and the community forms around it — drawn by harvest, health, and
honesty.

## Day 1 → Day 90 (the founder's sequence)

### Days 1–14: Observe
- Live on the land (or visit daily) through at least one season before cutting anything
- Map: water flow (where does rain go?), sun path, wind, existing trees, soil types
- Read `01-WATER/` now — water determines everything else
- Take photos from fixed points weekly; these become your change-record

### Days 15–40: Water Works
- Design and dig your first swales along contour (see `01-WATER/swales.md`)
- Set up rain catchment from any existing roof
- Plant the swale edges immediately with fast biomass species from `SPECIES/`

### Days 41–60: Soil Factory
- Start compost piles (multiple, at the future garden site)
- Begin chop-and-drop biomass cycles
- Plant the first guild (canopy + support species) from your climate driver

### Days 61–90: First Shelter Decision
- Evaluate earthbag/cob with materials from your own land (`04-SHELTER/`)
- Check `10-LAW-NAVIGATION/` for your jurisdiction BEFORE building
- Introduce yourself to neighbors and county officials — early, openly, with
  your plans printed out

### Ongoing from Day 1
- Keep the public ledger (even for one person) — `12-ECONOMY/`
- Teach what you do: photos, notes, mesh posts. Teaching is recruitment.
- File adaptation reports back to this repo

## The First Three People

- Person 2 and 3 arrive drawn by work and harvest, not ideology
- They sign the Covenant (`09-GOVERNANCE/COVENANT.md`) as equals from day one
- Facilitation rotates immediately — never let "founder" become a role with power

## When You're Stuck

- Every module has a troubleshooting section
- Ask the network: `PROTOCOLS/` explains how to connect to other communities
- Someone, somewhere, has already solved your exact problem — the protocols
  exist so their answer reaches you
